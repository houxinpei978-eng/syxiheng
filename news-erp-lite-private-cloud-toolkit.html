Custom ERP Lite Private Cloud Toolkit Released

Published: April 2026Category: Product Delivery

Shiyan Xiheng Technology introduced a private cloud deployment toolkit for Custom ERP Lite, enabling faster environment provisioning and safer rollout workflows for enterprise IT teams.

Toolkit Components

The toolkit includes baseline architecture references, deployment checklists, integration conventions, and operational validation scripts for staging and production readiness.

Adoption Impact

Clients can reduce setup lead time and improve deployment quality when moving ERP modules into private cloud or hybrid infrastructure footprints.
