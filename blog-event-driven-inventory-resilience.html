Event-Driven Inventory Resilience in Omnichannel Systems

Published: April 2026Domain: Architecture

Inventory correctness degrades rapidly when channels publish events at different speeds. Event-driven design can absorb this variance when services use strict idempotency and replay-safe processing.

Resilience Techniques

Introduce deduplication keys, time-window guards, and deterministic stock adjustment routines. Persist event offsets for replay and audit continuity.

Monitoring Strategy

Track event lag, duplicate rates, and compensation volume to identify drift before stock integrity issues impact customer experience.
