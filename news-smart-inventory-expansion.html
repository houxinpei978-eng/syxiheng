Smart Inventory Gateway Expansion Program Announced

Published: March 2026Category: Product Program

Shiyan Xiheng Technology has expanded its Smart Inventory Gateway implementation program to support broader retail and logistics use cases, including dense urban warehousing and distributed storefront networks.

Program Enhancements

The program now includes standardized deployment templates for scanner fleets, IoT shelf sensors, and dashboard configurations. This approach reduces rollout friction and improves consistency during multi-site activation.

Operational Value

Customers benefit from clearer replenishment signals, improved stock event traceability, and faster reaction windows in high-turnover categories. The expansion supports both pilot-first and full-region deployment models.
