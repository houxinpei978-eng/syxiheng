Omni-Commerce Suite Receives Multi-Currency Enhancements

Published: February 2026Category: Product Update

Shiyan Xiheng Technology has upgraded the multi-currency capabilities of its Omni-Commerce Suite to improve settlement consistency and operational transparency in international commerce workflows.

Update Highlights

The release introduces stronger currency conversion governance, unified settlement records, and improved reporting consistency across storefront and finance interfaces.

Client Benefits

Organizations operating in multiple regions can now align pricing, order settlement, and financial review pipelines with lower reconciliation friction and clearer audit trails.
