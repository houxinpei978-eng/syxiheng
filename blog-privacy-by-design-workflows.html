Building Privacy by Design into Product Workflows

Published: April 2026Domain: Compliance

Privacy by design works best when integrated at architecture time, not audit time. Product teams should model data categories, processing purposes, and retention paths before implementation begins.

Workflow Controls

Define consent boundaries, role-based access, and data minimization rules directly in system requirements. Build logging for purpose-based processing visibility and incident response readiness.

Outcome

Teams can reduce compliance friction, accelerate release approvals, and increase user trust by making privacy controls a standard engineering practice.
