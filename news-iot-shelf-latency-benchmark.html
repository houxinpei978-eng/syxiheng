IoT Shelf Latency Benchmark Results Published

Published: April 2026Category: Engineering Benchmark

Shiyan Xiheng Technology released internal benchmark outcomes for Smart Inventory Gateway deployments, focusing on shelf-event latency and high-frequency signal handling under typical retail traffic conditions.

Benchmark Scope

Scenarios covered normal and burst event rates, edge-to-cloud transmission stability, and dashboard update intervals. Results show reliable event delivery with controlled latency in sustained operation windows.

Why It Matters

Latency and signal consistency are core to replenishment timing and stockout prevention. This benchmark helps customers estimate rollout behavior and capacity planning confidence.
