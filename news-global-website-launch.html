Shiyan Xiheng Technology Launches Global Website and Policy Center

Published: April 2026 Category: Corporate Milestone

Shiyan Xiheng Technology has launched its new global corporate website, establishing a unified digital presence for international audiences. The release includes service pages, capability profiles, product information, and legal policy documentation structured for search discovery and cross-market trust building.

What Was Delivered

The launch package includes semantic HTML architecture, structured metadata, Open Graph tags, and legal pages for privacy and service terms. A sitemap and robots policy were introduced to support search indexing efficiency and crawler clarity.

Business Significance

This release sets a reliable content foundation for future product announcements, case studies, and technical thought leadership. It also supports lead generation with clearer information architecture and standardized global messaging.
